04 / Biography

"I move between the runway, the screen and the stage — turning bold ideas into work that feels effortless and unforgettable."

Born between two cultures, Anastensia won The Face Thailand (Season 5) — a major national series — and has since walked runways, fronted campaigns for global brands, and appeared on magazine covers including LIPS.

A singer with her own releases, she has also starred in music videos for some of Thailand's biggest artists — taking the lead in Polycat's "ดูดี", a hit with millions of views — and appeared in videos for DABOYWAY and UK rapper K-Trap's "Whistle". In 2026 she reached new audiences on Tower of Love.
